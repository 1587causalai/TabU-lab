2026-10-23Gains & tradeoffs

Regression improves; loss balancing adds no established benefit

Does scaling raw-value regression MSE by Support variance improve shared-model training in V8.4.0.2?

Retrospective · Published 2026-10-24

Protocol

Restore the same complete step-1059 state. Train raw and scaled arms on 200 matched episodes each; extend scaled by 200 updates under the predeclared validation rule. Total: 600 updates and 25.680 effective training minutes; selected path ends at step 1459. Temperature stays at 4.060441; historical test follows selection locking.

Results

The matched validation-score gap is only 0.000022747. Regression test R² over 12 tables rises from 0.623909 to 0.633948 (9 wins, 3 losses), versus 0.600851 for F19018. Mean binary AUC and multiclass logloss improve slightly, while accuracy decreases for both classification groups.

Failure & lesson

Most regression updates hit clip1 in both arms, attenuating the effect of scalar loss reweighting. One noise world accounts for about 79.5% of the new-world mean R² gain while median R² falls. Inspect effective updates, per-table behavior and retention.

Limits

The repeatedly used real48 panel is not an independent unseen-task evaluation. The 42 same-family development worlds informed selection. No raw400 control exists for the extension, so its gain cannot be attributed to balancing. Broad generalization has not been established.

Decision at the time

Retain the step-1459 candidate and both controls. Training stopped; project defaults are unchanged.
