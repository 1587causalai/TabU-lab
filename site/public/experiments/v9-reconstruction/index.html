Reconstruction error halves; new-observation risk barely moves

Can controlled Unit–Cell interaction in V9 learn reusable structure at lower cost?

Retrospective · Published 2026-10-24

Protocol

Three seeds × three readouts × 128 updates: 1,152 updates, 76.612 effective training seconds. Full-observation reconstruction on 64-row, 8-column numeric tables. The model reads only observation A; independent-noise observation B and the latent mechanism are used only for scoring. Final, calibration and monitoring tables use distinct mechanism parameters and individual seeds.

Results

Monitoring code MSE falls by 46–53%, while expected new-noise NMSE changes by roughly −0.1% to +1%. Projected final risk is about 1.51% above bilinear. All three heads underperform a column-mean baseline on independent columns. At 2,048 rows projected is about 8.3× faster in a readout-only microbenchmark, not end-to-end training.

Failure & lesson

Self-reconstruction and prediction on a new observation need separate evidence. A visible-answer path can lower reconstruction loss without producing the intended statistical learning. A cheaper readout does not automatically predict better.

Limits

Three seeds, small numeric tables, and new parameters within existing function families only. Training time excludes compilation, calibration and evaluation. Readout timing is not whole-network timing. Unseen mechanisms, real tables and large-network behavior were not tested.

Decision at the time

Keep projected as a cost candidate. No quality advantage is established; learning objectives and independent observations remain the next research question.
